# Hoops Trainer

Interactive trainer for our 6th grade rec basketball team. Works on phones, iPads and computers.

- **Press & Press Break:** box press break, 2-2-1 zone press (force left, trap the sideline, rotate), good trap form
- **Half-Court Sets:** 4-Out Motion vs man, 1-3-1 vs zone, man-to-man help defense, 2-3 zone, and league-rule strategy

Open a trainer directly by adding `#press` or `#half` to the site link.

Everything is in one file, `index.html`, which loads nothing from the internet. To update the site, replace `index.html`.
